FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How do I place an order?

Pick a colour and size on any product, then tap Order on WhatsApp. A message with your choice opens ready to send to the store.

How do I choose my size?

Use the size finder on the home page, or the size guide at the bottom of each category page. Our shirts are cut loose and boxy, so size down if you want a closer fit.

Can I change my colour or size after I message?

Yes. Just tell the store on WhatsApp before your order is confirmed.

How do delivery, payment and exchanges work?

Message the store on WhatsApp to confirm delivery, payment and exchange details for your order.
